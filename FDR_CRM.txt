KRA formula (per agent per month):
  Production  = SUM(volume × weight) / workingDays / targetPerDay   → 60 % weight
  Quality     = qualityScore / 5                                      → 30 % weight
  Shrinkage   = 1 − (plannedShrinkage + unplannedShrinkage)          → 10 % weight
  Overall     = Production×0.6 + Quality×0.3 + Shrinkage×0.1
  Rating      = 1 (< 60 %) | 2 (60–69 %) | 3 (70–79 %) | 4 (80–89 %) | 5 (≥ 90 %)
  Target Met  = Overall ≥ 0.80
